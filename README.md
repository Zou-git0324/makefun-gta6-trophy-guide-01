GTA VI Trophy Guide & Tracker — a free, open-source, offline guide to all 55 GTA 6 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), with a checklist saved in your browser, a 6-step roadmap to the platinum, and a guide to every trophy in our own words, each citing its sources.

What it does:

- **Tracker over all 55 official trophies** — name, description and grade for each, checked against the official list as published on PSN (18 November 2026).
- **Checklist saved in the browser** — tick trophies off as you earn them; progress survives reloads, with export and import so you can back it up or move it to another machine.
- **Filters by grade and kind** (story, activity, strangers, collectibles, crime, exploration, completion, misc), plus search across names, descriptions and guides, and a status filter for earned / still to earn.
- **Spoiler setting** — hides the 12 story trophies (and the hidden ones) until you earn them, so the guide never spoils the story.
- **The one missable flagged** — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy PowerPyx and GTABase flag as possibly missable, because the first meditation spot can only be used early in Chapter 1. It is flagged in the list and in the roadmap.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** — six steps: play the story, protect the missable, try all 9 activity types, finish the stranger strands, clean up with the in-game 100% checklist, then the platinum. Platinum estimate: about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx).
- **A guide to each trophy, in our own words, citing its sources** — what to do, where, and when it can be missed. Where a method is still unconfirmed (The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin), the guide says so instead of guessing.
- **Sources section** — PowerPyx, Push Square, GTABase, PSNProfiles and KeenGamer, with what each one was used for. No guide text is copied from any of them.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Static files only; it loads nothing over the network and your checklist never leaves your machine.
